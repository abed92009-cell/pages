What MirrorMirror is

MirrorMirror puts your Mac's clipboard on your phone. Copy on the Mac and it is on the phone. Copy on the phone and it is on the Mac. There is no account and no cloud: your devices talk to each other over your own private network.

Getting started

  • Run the MirrorMirror service on your Mac.
  • Open the app on your phone and tap Pair.
  • Scan the code your Mac shows. That is the whole setup.

Before you pair anything

The app opens in Demo mode with sample data, so you can see what it does without a Mac in front of you. Demo mode makes no network request at all.

Common questions

It says Demo and will not show my clipboard. The app has not been paired yet. Tap Pair and scan the code on your Mac.

It cannot find my Mac. Both devices need to be on the same private network. Check the Mac service is running.

Where is my data kept? On your own devices only. See the privacy page.

How do I remove everything? Delete the app to remove the phone's copy, and stop the Mac service to remove the Mac's.

Contact
