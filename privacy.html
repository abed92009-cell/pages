MirrorMirror privacy

Last updated 9 September 2026

MirrorMirror collects nothing about you, because there is nowhere for it to go.

There is no account, no sign-in, no server of ours and no cloud. Your Mac and your phone talk to each other directly over your own private network. We never see any of it, and neither does anyone else.

What the app holds

  • What you copied, on your own devices, so you can paste it again.
  • What Crunch has processed, and the token counts it saved, on your own devices.
  • The pairing details for your own Mac, on your own phone.

All of it stays on hardware you own. Deleting the app removes the phone's copy. Deleting the Mac service removes the Mac's.

What leaves your devices

Nothing. The app makes no request to any address that is not your own Mac on your own network.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. There is no analytics library, no advertising library, and no identifier for advertisers. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and there is no third party in the app to track you on their behalf.

Children

The app shows only what its owner copied on their own machine. It has no messaging, no user-generated content shared between people, no purchases and no browsing.

Demo mode

Before you pair a Mac, the app runs in Demo mode with sample data that ships inside the app. Demo mode makes no network request at all.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the change is written in the app's own change log rather than only here.

Contact

MirrorMirror is made in New Zealand by MAG. Reach the developer through the support link on the app's store page.
